Data protection

Personal data protection policy

Colegio Ejemplo, tax ID 000.000.000-0, registered at Calle 00 # 00 - 00, Ciudad, Departamento. Contact address for data protection matters: info@colegioejemplo.edu.co.

The data you provide through the forms on this site is used solely to respond to your enquiry, to carry out the admissions process and to send you the institutional information you have requested.

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising.
  • We keep the information only for as long as it is needed for those purposes.

Under Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1074 of 2015, you may access, update, correct and delete your personal data, and withdraw the consent you have given. To exercise these rights, write to info@colegioejemplo.edu.co.

Publishing photographs or videos of students under 18 requires prior, express written consent from their parents or guardians. That consent is voluntary, specific and may be withdrawn at any time.
