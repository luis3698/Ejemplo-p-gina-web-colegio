What sets us apart

Four convictions behind everything we do

They are not a poster on the wall: they are the criteria behind every pedagogical decision we make.

Active learning

Students build their knowledge by doing, asking and getting things wrong. Teachers accompany that process with intent and method.

The whole person

Academic, emotional, physical, artistic and civic development happen at the same time. No dimension replaces another.

Small groups

Every teacher knows their students by name, by history and by the particular way each one learns. Follow-up is personal.

Family and school

Education requires consistency between home and school. Families take part through clear channels and regular meetings.

Our approach

Learning is an experience, not a list of topics

Our institutional educational project combines the official Colombian curriculum with active learning. The content is there — and it is covered — but students reach it through experience: the project, the question, the laboratory, the workshop.

The result is students who keep their curiosity, who know how to work with others and who arrive at upper secondary able to think for themselves.

  • Prepared environments by age, with materials and spaces designed for each stage.
  • Process-based assessment: continuous observation, portfolio and conversation with the family.
  • Emotional support and conflict resolution as part of the curriculum.
  • English and the arts from preschool onwards.

The campus

Spaces designed for learning

Bright, generous classrooms, science laboratories, a library, a computer room, art studios, a dining hall and sports areas. Every space has a defined pedagogical purpose.

The campus is walkable and designed so that moving between spaces is a natural part of the school day.

  • Science laboratories and a computer room.
  • Library and reading rooms by level.
  • Studios for visual arts, music and drama.
  • Sports courts, dining hall and school transport.
