Admissions

Start the process for the coming school year

Our admissions process is not a selection exam: it is a conversation. We want to get to know your family, and for your family to get to know us, so that we can tell together whether this is the right project for you.

Fill in the application See the process

Step by step

Five steps, no surprises

The whole process can be completed in two or three weeks, depending on your family's availability.

  1. Request information

    Fill in the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. We will send you the information pack with our educational project and current fees.

  2. Campus visit

    A guided tour during the school day, with the whole family. This is where the questions no brochure answers get resolved.

  3. Family interview

    A conversation with the head of school and the coordination team about your family's expectations and the applicant's school history.

  4. Trial day

    The applicant spends a day with the group they would join. It is not an exam: it is a chance for them to feel comfortable and for us to see how they relate to others.

  5. Decision and enrolment

    We communicate the decision in writing. If there is a place and we agree, the enrolment is formalised with the submission of documents.

Requirements

Documents for enrolment

Documents are submitted once a place is confirmed. To begin the process you only need the form.

From the student

  • Birth certificate or identity card (copy).
  • Reports from the current and previous school year.
  • Withdrawal certificate from the previous school.
  • Vaccination record (preschool) and current health insurance certificate.
  • Two recent passport-style photographs.
  • Clearance certificate from the previous school.

From the family

  • Identity document of the parent or guardian (copy).
  • Signed enrolment contract and payment undertaking.
  • Personal data processing consent.
  • Image-use consent (optional and revocable).
  • Acceptance of the code of conduct.

Financial aid

We set aside part of our resources for financial support for families in the region. Applications are reviewed case by case, based on need and on commitment to the educational project.

Sibling discount

Families with more than one child enrolled receive a discount on the tuition of the second child onwards. Ask the administration office about the conditions.
