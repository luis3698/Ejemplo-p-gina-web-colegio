School life

What happens between lessons teaches too

Arts, sport, field trips, celebrations and assemblies. Daily school life is part of the curriculum, not an add-on.

The place

A campus designed for school life

Bright, generous classrooms, laboratories, a library, art studios, a dining hall, courts and green areas. Spaces are organised by level so that each age has its own territory within the school.

The whole campus is walkable and designed so that moving between spaces is a natural part of the day.

  • Classrooms by level, with materials and furniture suited to each age.
  • Science laboratories and a computer room.
  • Library, art studios and dining hall.
  • Sports courts and green areas for free play.
  • Nurse's room and spaces for counselling support.

Programs and projects

Beyond the timetable

Research groups

Groups of students who explore one topic across the year and present it at the school's project fair.

Arts and drama

Visual arts, music and drama throughout the year, with a showcase open to the community at the end of each semester.

Field trips

Day trips by level and multi-day residentials for secondary students: independence, community life and learning outside the classroom.

Developmental sport

Cooperative games, athletics and team sports, with participation valued above results.

Student government

Student ombudsperson, student council and assemblies: students take part in real decisions about school life.

Parents' school

Sessions and conversations with parents and carers about parenting, boundaries and how to support their children.

Services

What the school takes care of for your family

School transport

Routes with an adult attendant on board and timings coordinated with the school day.

Dining hall

Lunch and snacks prepared on campus, with menus reviewed by a nutritionist.

Counselling support

Support for students' emotional development and guidance for families when needed.

Communication with families

Circulars, termly meetings and a direct channel to each student's form tutor.

Spend a day at the school

We welcome interested families during the school day, so they can see the school as it really works, with nothing staged.
