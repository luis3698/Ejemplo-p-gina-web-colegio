About us

Three decades supporting our students' growth

We are an independent calendar-A school. Since {{founded}} we have worked from one simple conviction: children and young people learn best when they feel seen, challenged and accompanied by well-prepared adults.

Principles

Six agreements that hold school life together

The criteria behind the pedagogical decisions we make every day.

01

Respect for pace

Everyone learns at a different speed. We accompany that pace instead of forcing it, without giving up on rigour or on the standards expected for each grade.

02

Autonomy within clear limits

Freedom is not the absence of rules. We build agreements together with students and uphold them with firmness and warmth.

03

Relationship before content

Nobody learns from someone who doesn't see them. Teachers know each student, and that relationship is the foundation of the academic work.

04

Critical thinking

We teach students to build an argument, to compare sources and to change their mind when the evidence calls for it.

05

Families are part of it

A parents' school, regular meetings and participation in projects. Education requires consistency between home and school.

06

Roots and openness

We value our community and prepare students to engage with the wider world from that foundation.

Our history

From a small project to a school community

  1. Year 1

    The school is founded

    A group of families and educators founds the school with the idea of offering a different pedagogical alternative in the region.

  2. Year 8

    Primary school completed

    The preschool project extends through fifth grade and a curriculum of our own takes shape.

  3. Year 15

    A new campus

    The school moves to its current site, with its own laboratories, library and sports areas.

  4. Year 22

    Full secondary school

    Upper secondary opens. Students who arrived in kindergarten can now graduate in the same place where they learned to read.

  5. Today

    More than 600 students

    The community grows and results in external exams stay above the national average.
